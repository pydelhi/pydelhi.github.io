This covers:

- Meetup and event photos: `src/assets/photos/`, `src/assets/events/`, `src/assets/blog/` and `src/assets/team/`
- Videos of PyDelhi events
- PyDelhi's logos and wordmark: `src/assets/brand/`, `src/assets/logos/pydelhi.svg` and the icons and share card in `src/assets/icons/`

## Not covered

Every other file in `src/assets/logos/` is the logo of a company that has hosted, sponsored or employed a speaker at PyDelhi. Those logos belong to their owners and are shown only to credit them. They aren't ours to license.

People in the photos keep their own rights to their likeness. To have a photo of yourself taken down, email [contact@pydelhi.org](mailto:contact@pydelhi.org).
